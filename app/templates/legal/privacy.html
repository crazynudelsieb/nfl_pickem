Privacy & data transparency

{{ config.APP_NAME }} is a private pick'em league tracker. It keeps an account for you and records the picks you make so it can score them and build the standings — this page lays out exactly what is kept and what isn't.

{# --- What the app never does --------------------------------------- #}

What {{ config.APP_NAME }} never does

  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking or profiling scripts.
  • No tracking cookies; your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • No real-money wagering, no payment data, and no financial details of any kind.
{# --- What is stored ------------------------------------------------ #}

What is stored

To provide the service, the app keeps only:

  • Your account — username, email address, and a salted hash of your password. The password itself is never stored and cannot be recovered, only reset.
  • Your picks and their results — the team picked each week, points scored, and tiebreaker margins, kept for the season history and all-time standings.
  • Group membership — which leagues you belong to and your role in them.
  • Admin audit entries when a league admin makes or changes a pick on someone's behalf, so such changes stay accountable.
{# --- Cookies ------------------------------------------------------- #}

Cookies

One strictly-necessary, signed session cookie (HttpOnly). It keeps you logged in and protects forms against CSRF. It carries no tracking identifiers, so no consent banner is required.

{# --- Third-party assets -------------------------------------------- #}

Third-party assets and data

Interface icons, scripts, and the real-time connection are loaded from public CDNs (cdnjs, Socket.IO). Your browser fetches these files directly, so those services may see your IP address as part of delivering them. They receive no other data from {{ config.APP_NAME }}.

Schedules, scores, and team logos come from ESPN's public NFL API. That data is fetched by the server on a schedule — never by your browser — so no request of yours is passed on to ESPN, and no personal data ever leaves this instance for them.

{# --- Email --------------------------------------------------------- #}

Email

Your email address is used only for account business — password resets and league invitations — sent through the operator's mail server. It is used for nothing else, and there is no newsletter or marketing mail.

{% endif %} {# --- Data at rest -------------------------------------------------- #}

Data at rest

Apart from password hashes, data is stored unencrypted at the application level so picks can be scored and standings computed. Protecting the database at rest (disk or managed-database encryption) is the responsibility of whoever operates this instance.

{# --- Your control -------------------------------------------------- #}

Your data, your control

  • Review your full pick history and season results at any time from your profile.
  • Update your username, email, and password from your profile page.
  • For account deletion, or an export of your data, contact the operator. Deletion is irreversible in the live database, though operator backups may retain a copy until they age out.
