📖 Game Rules

Learn how to play and win at NFL Pick'em

🎯 Regular Season Rules

One Pick Per Week

Each game week, you select ONE team that you believe will win their game. You can change your pick until the game you picked kicks off.

No Repeat Teams

During the regular season (Weeks 1-18), each team can only be picked once. Teams reset for the playoffs, so choose strategically!

No Repeat Opponents

You cannot pick against the same opponent two weeks in a row. If your pick faced Team X last week, this week's pick cannot be playing against Team X.

Group Settings:

These are the default rules. Group admins can adjust them per group in the group settings: the repeat-team and repeat-opponent rules can be switched off, and the number of playoff and Super Bowl spots can be changed.

🏆 Scoring System

✅ Correct Pick

  • +1 point for the win
  • +Margin of victory as tiebreaker points
  • Example: Team wins by 14? You get +1 win, +14 tiebreaker

❌ Incorrect Pick

  • 0 points - no win credit
  • -Margin of loss as tiebreaker penalty
  • Example: Team loses by 7? You get 0 wins, -7 tiebreaker

🤝 Tie Game

  • +0.5 points if your game ends in a tie
  • No tiebreaker change (no margin of victory)

⏰ Missed Week

  • 0 points - no pick, no win
  • Missed weeks count against your accuracy
How Tiebreakers Work:

Tiebreaker points only matter when two or more players have the same number of wins. The player with the higher tiebreaker total ranks higher. This rewards picking teams that win decisively and penalizes picking teams that lose badly.

🏅 Playoffs & Super Bowl

Who Makes the Playoffs?

After the regular season (18 weeks), the top 4 players advance to the playoffs (group admins can change the number of spots). Rankings are determined by:

  1. Total wins (most important)
  2. Tiebreaker points (if wins are equal)

Playoff Changes

  • Teams reset: Teams can be picked as often as you want, even if you used them in the regular season
  • Opponents reset: The repeat-opponent rule does not apply in the playoffs
  • Separate scoring: Your regular season score is frozen; playoff standings count playoff wins, with season-long tiebreaker points deciding ties

The Super Bowl

  • Only the top 2 players of the playoff standings may pick in the Super Bowl (group admins can change the number of spots)
  • Each qualifier picks any team they like - no restrictions

💡 Strategy Tips

🎯 Save Strong Teams

Don't use all the best teams early. Save powerhouse teams for difficult weeks when other options look weak.

📅 Plan Ahead

Look at the upcoming schedule. If a great team has a tough matchup this week but an easy one next week, consider waiting.

⚖️ Consider Margins

When tied in wins, tiebreaker points decide rankings. Picking teams in blowouts helps, but be careful - big losses hurt your tiebreaker!

🚫 Watch the Opponent Rule

Remember: you can't pick against the same opponent two weeks in a row. Check who your pick is facing before you lock it in!
